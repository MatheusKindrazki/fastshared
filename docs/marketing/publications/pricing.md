## The details

### Free tier
Free stays free. We designed the Free tier so you can test the delivery workflow on real client projects without entering a credit card.

- **One active publication:** Keep one live deliverable online at any time.
- **Seven-day lifespan:** Deliverables automatically expire seven days after publishing. Perfect for short review cycles.
- **Three open notifications:** Receive push notifications to your iPhone or Mac for the first three times your link is rendered in a browser.
- **Hosted badge:** Publications display a small, unobtrusive footer badge: "Hosted with FastShared".
- **Zero friction:** Publish immediately via web drag-and-drop or command line.

### Pro tier
Built for active consultants, freelancers, and technical agencies shipping client deliverables every week.

- **US$15 billed monthly, or US$144 billed annually** (saving US$36 every year).
- **Multiple active deliverables:** Host all your current client projects within a 2 GB total storage allocation.
- **Durable hosting:** Links stay live for as long as your membership is active.
- **Unlimited open alerts:** Never miss an opening, whether the link is opened today, next week, or next month.
- **Clean white-label presentation:** No FastShared footer badge on your client deliverables.
- **Predictable cancellation safety:** If you cancel your subscription, your publications remain online for 30 days while we notify you and allow you to export your files. Nothing is suddenly deleted.

## Roadmap and feature transparency

We believe in strict truth in advertising. Features that are under active development or scheduled for our second release cycle (P2) are marked as **Coming soon**:

- **Same-URL versioning:** The ability to push an updated version of a publication to the existing URL without breaking client bookmarks.

- **Detailed view analytics:** Time spent on page and breakdown by version.
- **MCP server integration:** Publishing and updating deliverables directly from Claude Desktop, Cursor, or AI terminal agents.

These features will roll out directly to Pro subscribers as they clear quality verification.

## Frequently asked questions about pricing

### Does the recipient pay anything?
No. Your clients never pay, never see a paywall, and do not need to register. They simply view your live deliverable in their web browser.

### What happens if I cancel Pro?
If you decide to cancel, your publications do not vanish immediately. You receive 30 days of advance notice and access to download your deliverables. After 30 days, files are permanently deleted from storage.

### Can I revoke a link before it expires?
Yes. On both Free and Pro, you can revoke any publication immediately from your dashboard or terminal. Once revoked, the link immediately stops serving content.

### Is there a Team plan?
Not at this time. We are focused on crafting the ideal solo workflow for independent consultants and freelancers. We will introduce team collaboration only when our customers ask for it.
