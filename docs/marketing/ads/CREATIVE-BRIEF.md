# FastShared — Creative Brief

Voice anchor: `docs/marketing/voice-tone.md` (Confident · Minimal · Poetic).
**Do not deviate.** The product's whole value prop is restraint — ads must
mirror that or they undercut the experience.

---

## 3 angles to ship for Phase 1

### Angle A — Vanish

> **"Share anything. Get a link. Watch it vanish."**

- **Insight:** files outliving their purpose is the modern annoyance —
  Drive folders no one cleans, WhatsApp media filling phones.
- **Visual:** real iOS share sheet → upload progress → countdown timer
  ticking down → file dissolves.
- **Closing frame:** logo + "fastsha.red" + App Store badge.

### Angle B — By design

> **"Every link expires. Every file is deleted. By design."**

- **Insight:** privacy isn't a feature stack, it's a default. Frame the
  constraint as the product.
- **Visual:** black background, white text, type-on. No product UI at all.
  One ambient note (flute or sine wave). 6s.
- **Closing frame:** logo only.

### Angle C — Speed

> **"Faster than email. Smaller than Drive."**

- **Insight:** the existing tools are bloated for a 30-second task.
- **Visual:** split screen — left side, someone struggling with email
  attachment limit. Right side, fastshared share extension, one tap, done.
- **Closing frame:** "for things that don't need to live forever" + App
  Store badge.

## Production specs

### Meta (Reels + Stories)

| Spec | Value |
|---|---|
| Aspect ratio | **9:16** (1080×1920) |
| Length | **6–12s** (Reels punishes >15s on cold traffic) |
| Format | MP4, H.264, ≤4GB |
| Safe zone | Top 14% + bottom 20% reserved (UI overlays) — keep all text and CTAs in middle 66% |
| Audio | Required (TikTok-style sound-off rules don't apply here, but caption-burn anyway for accessibility) |
| Captions | Burned in, not file-attached (Meta's auto-captions are unreliable for PT-BR) |
| End card | Last 1.5s: logo + App Store badge |
| Variants needed | 3 angles × 1 cut = **3 videos minimum** |

### ASA Custom Product Pages (CPP)

ASA doesn't run video ads — it shows your App Store screenshots. CPPs let
you show different screenshots per ad group.

| CPP | Used by ad group | Screenshots focus |
|---|---|---|
| **Speed CPP** | Category (gesture-related keywords) | Frame 1: share sheet w/ fastshared. Frame 2: instant link. Frame 3: countdown. Frame 4: "Faster than email" callout |
| **Privacy CPP** | Category (privacy-related keywords) | Frame 1: link expiry timer. Frame 2: "Every link expires." Frame 3: "Every file is deleted." Frame 4: "By design." |
| Default page | Brand + Discovery | Existing App Store listing |

**Screenshot spec:** 1290×2796 (iPhone 15/16 Pro Max), 6.7" required slot.
PNG, sRGB, ≤500KB each.

## What to avoid (hard NO)

From the voice doc + ad-platform reality:

- ❌ Stock music with drops, builds, or "epic" tones — kills the poetic register
- ❌ Voiceover. We have no spokesperson. Text + ambient sound only.
- ❌ Emojis in copy. Anywhere.
- ❌ Hedging words: "easily", "simply", "instantly" — voice doc forbids
- ❌ "Cloud storage" framing — we are explicitly NOT a cloud drive
- ❌ Comparison ads naming WeTransfer/Send Anywhere by name (legal risk +
  off-brand)
- ❌ AI-generated B-roll of "happy users sharing files" — generic AI
  aesthetic destroys positioning. Real screen recordings only.

## Production approach (zero-budget mode)

You don't have a video budget. That's fine. Here's the cheap path:

1. **Tools:** QuickTime screen recording on iPhone 15 Pro (iOS 17+) +
   CapCut (free) for cuts and burned captions.
2. **Music:** strip audio from EpidemicSound 14-day trial, OR use Apple's
   royalty-free ambient stems from GarageBand.
3. **Time budget:** 4 hours per angle = 12 hours total. Don't over-polish.
4. **Iteration:** plan to **replace 1 video per week** based on Meta's
   creative ranking. Fresh creative >> polished creative when budget is
   tight.
