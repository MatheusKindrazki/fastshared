What's new in 1.0.3

• On Mac, FastShared now starts automatically at login and waits quietly in the menu bar, ready the moment you need to share. You can turn this off anytime in Settings.
• The sidebar now lists all of your iCloud devices, so it's clearer where your links and uploads are coming from.
• Reliability fixes for iCloud sync and Settings, plus general polish.

As always: share anything from any app, choose how long the link lives, and the short URL is on your clipboard before the upload even finishes. Every file is temporary on purpose — links expire on schedule and the media is deleted automatically.
