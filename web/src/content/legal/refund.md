## 1. Overview

At FastShared, we strive to build a fast, dependable, and transparent utility for sharing files and publishing web deliverables. We want you to be completely satisfied with your FastShared Pro subscription.

This Refund and Cancellation Policy outlines your rights and the procedures for canceling your subscription and requesting refunds. Because FastShared is distributed across two distinct platforms, your payment processor and refund administrator depend on where you purchased your subscription:

1. **Web Purchases (`fastsha.red`):** Processed by **Paddle.com**, acting as Merchant of Record.
2. **Apple App Store Purchases (iOS / iPadOS / macOS apps):** Processed by **Apple Inc.**, acting as Merchant of Record.

## 2. Web Subscriptions (Purchased via Paddle)

For purchases completed on the FastShared website (`web/pricing` or account management dashboard):

> **Our order process is conducted by our online reseller Paddle.com. Paddle.com is the Merchant of Record for all our orders. Paddle provides all customer service inquiries and handles returns.**

### 2.1. 14-Day Money-Back Guarantee
If you subscribe to **FastShared Pro Monthly** or **FastShared Pro Annual** via the web and decide the service does not meet your expectations, you are entitled to a full refund if you submit your request within **fourteen (14) calendar days** of the initial transaction date.
- This 14-day policy fulfills and exceeds statutory withdrawal periods, including the European Union and UK Consumer Rights statutory 14-day cooling-off period and the Brazilian Consumer Protection Code (*Art. 49 do Código de Defesa do Consumidor — direito de arrependimento*).
- Discretionary refunds requested after the 14-day window are evaluated on a case-by-case basis (e.g., in the event of extended service outages or verified technical failures).

### 2.2. Cancellation Policy
- **Cancel Anytime:** You may cancel your subscription at any time without penalty or cancellation fees.
- **How to Cancel:** You can cancel self-service through the **Paddle Customer Portal** (accessible via the link in your purchase receipt email or from your FastShared account settings) or by emailing `support@fastsha.red`.
- **Access Retained:** When you cancel an auto-renewing subscription, your Pro tier access remains active until the end of your current paid billing period (month or year). Your subscription will not renew, and no further recurring charges will be made.
- **Post-Cancellation 30-Day Notice Window:** After your paid period ends, your web publications remain online for **30 calendar days** under a protective notice window. You will receive email reminders to export your files. At the conclusion of this 30-day period, publications transition to expired and the files are permanently deleted.

### 2.3. How to Request a Refund (Web Purchases)
To request a refund for a web purchase:
1. **Option A (FastShared Support — Recommended):** Email `support@fastsha.red` from the email address associated with your purchase, including your order number or Paddle transaction ID. We typically process eligible requests within 1–2 business days.
2. **Option B (Paddle Buyer Support):** Contact Paddle directly via their 24/7 buyer portal at [paddle.net](https://paddle.net) or email `help@paddle.com`.

### 2.4. Refund Processing and Payout Methods
- Once approved, refunds are credited back to the original payment method used at checkout:
  - **Credit / Debit Cards:** Typically reflect on your statement within 3 to 5 business days, depending on your card issuer.
  - **Pix (Brazil):** Refunds are routed through Paddle's local payment partner back to the originating Brazilian bank account linked to the buyer's CPF/CNPJ within 1 to 3 business days.
  - **Apple Pay (Web):** Processed back to the underlying linked payment card.

## 3. In-App Purchases (Purchased via Apple App Store)

If you purchased FastShared Pro within the iOS, iPadOS, or macOS applications:
- **Merchant of Record:** Apple Inc. is the legal merchant of record for all App Store transactions and manages all billing, taxes, payment collection, and refunds under Apple's standard terms.
- **FastShared Limitations:** FastShared does not possess your payment details and cannot initiate, approve, or issue refunds for App Store transactions directly.

### 3.1. How to Request an Apple Refund
To request a refund for an in-app purchase:
1. Visit Apple's self-service portal at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).
2. Sign in with the Apple ID used to complete the purchase.
3. Select "I'd like to..." and choose **"Request a refund"**.
4. Choose the reason for your request and select your FastShared purchase.
5. Submit the claim directly to Apple.

Apple reviews refund requests against their own guidelines (typically within 48 hours). If Apple grants your refund, Apple’s servers notify FastShared via automated server-to-server notifications, and Pro access is revoked.

### 3.2. How to Cancel Apple Subscriptions
You can manage or cancel auto-renewal of your FastShared subscription at any time:
- **On iPhone or iPad:** Go to **Settings → [Your Name / Apple ID] → Subscriptions → FastShared → Cancel Subscription**.
- **On Mac:** Open the **App Store → Click your name / Account Settings → Manage Subscriptions → FastShared → Cancel Subscription**.
- You must cancel at least **24 hours** before the end of the current billing cycle to avoid being charged by Apple for the next period.

## 5. Chargebacks, Disputes, and Fraud

We encourage customers to contact us directly at `support@fastsha.red` or via `paddle.net` before initiating a formal bank chargeback or dispute. Most billing concerns, accidental renewals, or duplicate charges can be resolved immediately without dispute fees.

In cases where a fraudulent chargeback is initiated, associated device tokens and accounts will be suspended pending investigation.

## 6. Ineligible Situations / Non-Refundable Items

Refunds may be denied in the following circumstances:
- **Terms of Service Violations:** Accounts or publications terminated due to severe breaches of our [Terms of Service](/terms/) — including hosting Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM), distributing malware or ransomware, phishing, or engaging in credential harvesting — are strictly ineligible for refunds.
- **Repeat Requests:** Accounts demonstrating a pattern of repeated subscription, immediate mass usage/export, and repeated refund requests may be refused discretionary refunds.
- **Free Tier Usage:** Free tier usage incurs no charges, and no monetary refunds apply.
