This file is the single source of truth for design decisions on the FastShared marketing site (`web/`, deployed to `www.fastsha.red`). All `/impeccable` skills load this before doing any work.

Last updated: 2026-04-19

## Design Context

### Users

**Primary audience.** Designers and developers who share screenshots, quick mockups, exports, and PDFs many times a day and are tired of WhatsApp/Drive/Dropbox/AirDrop friction. They live on macOS and iOS, use Raycast/Things/Linear, and judge an app by its motion, typography, and how few taps it costs them. They will install a TestFlight build on the strength of one screenshot and a clean hero.

**Secondary audience.** iOS power users — the Shortcuts/AppIntents/automation crowd. They care that FastShared exposes a Siri Shortcut for "share most recent screenshot" and that the share-extension does the right thing in a single tap. They convert via word-of-mouth and HN/PH launch threads.

**Tertiary audience.** Press, HN, Product Hunt, and indie-dev Twitter visiting during launch week. They scan the hero, the pricing, and the press page and decide in ~10 seconds whether the project is worth a writeup or a retweet. The lifetime tier ($49.99 early-access window) is built specifically for this beat.

**Context of use.** Browsers on a laptop, often a second tab during work; a smaller share on iPhone via a shared link in Slack/Discord/Twitter. Lighting is variable; many users keep the OS in dark mode. Sessions are short (60–120s) and goal-directed: "is this the thing I think it is, and how do I get it."

**Job to be done.** Convince the visitor that FastShared is the fastest, most opinionated, most beautiful way to share an ephemeral file from iOS/macOS, then route them to TestFlight. The ephemerality story (links die) is the persuasion mechanism, not a side-feature — the click is the consequence of believing the story.

### Brand Personality

**Three words: ephemeral · opinionated · crafted.**

- **Ephemeral.** Every link expires. The product is built around that fact and the marketing has to feel the same — countdowns, fade-outs, a Dynamic Island that cycles `uploading → completed → expired` even on the static landing page. Nothing on this site should feel "permanent feature wall." Surfaces breathe and decay.
- **Opinionated.** One tap. No compression. No settings to fiddle. 3 uploads/day on free, $2.99/mo on Pro, $49.99 lifetime, take it or leave it. The voice on the site has the same posture: short sentences, declarative, no hedging, no "maybe this is for you."
- **Crafted.** Plane+Arc motion logo, Bricolage Grotesque + JetBrains Mono, CSS-only animation (no GSAP), reduced-motion respected as a first-class state. The site itself is evidence of the product's care.

**Emotional goal.** A designer/dev should feel a small "oh — these people get it" jolt within the first 4 seconds. Not "wow what a cool startup." Specifically the recognition of a peer's restraint.

### Aesthetic Direction

**Theme: dark.** The product is consumed by macOS/iOS users who keep their OS in dark mode and their hero has to live next to a Slack window without burning their retinas. Dark is also where the violet accent and the ambient glow read most cinematically. Light mode for the marketing site is explicitly out of scope.

**Palette (already committed in `tailwind.config.mjs` and `src/styles/global.css` — do not redefine, only consume):**

- **Background:** `--ink #070318` with two radial glows (deep purple `oklch(...)` at 18%/-10% and pink at 92%/18%) — establishes depth without gradient text or hero gradients on type.
- **Primary action:** `--violet-hot #9d7aff` — buttons, focus rings, the live ornaments inside the brand lockup. This is the only "loud" color and it must stay rare.
- **Secondary tints:** `--violet-soft #c1a9ff`, `--violet-fade #ff7ad1`, `--violet-dust #e0d4ff` — for hovers, halos, gradient SVG fills inside the Plane+Arc mark only.
- **Warm accent:** `--amber #ff9f47` and `--ember #ffc487` — section labels, step numbers (01/02/03), small icon tints. Amber is the "system text" color of the brand; violet is the "interactive" color. Do not blur this distinction.
- **Coral:** `--coral #ff4e7c` — used exactly once per page max, on the lifetime ribbon and on the "expired" Dynamic Island state. Coral signals "ending" and must keep that meaning.
- **Text:** `--milk #fafaff` (default), `--milk-dim`, `--milk-faint`, `--milk-ghost` for the descending hierarchy. Pure white is banned (already correct).

**Typography (already loaded in `src/layouts/Base.astro`):**

- **Display:** Bricolage Grotesque, with stylistic alternates `'ss02', 'cv11', 'ss01'` enabled. Used for the hero, section headlines, FAQ answers, pricing tier names. Letter-spacing is aggressive: `-0.045em` on standard headings, `-0.055em` on hero, occasional `-0.06em`.
- **Mono:** JetBrains Mono. Used for section labels, step numbers, numeric data ("100 MB", "24H"), nav links, footer, badges. All-caps is reserved for mono labels at 11–15px with `tracking-[0.08em]` to `tracking-[0.18em]`.
- **No third typeface.** Resist any urge to add a serif "for elegance" or a script "for warmth." The Bricolage + JetBrains pairing is the entire system.

**References (taste anchors, not templates):**

- **Linear's marketing era ~2022–2023** — the version with restrained motion, opinionated typography, and small functional interface mockups embedded in the page. Specifically the "small details, lots of confidence" register.
- **Things 3 product page** — for the way it shows interface fragments at real scale instead of generic dashboard screenshots.
- **Apple's iOS feature pages** — for the Dynamic Island treatment specifically. The way Apple uses the Island as a narrative device is the reference for our rotating `uploading → completed → expired` mockup.
- **Pitch's typography** — for confident clamp() display sizing and tight tracking.

What we are emphatically taking from these: posture, restraint, and motion economy. What we are not taking: any specific layout, color, or component shape. Direct visual mimicry would dilute the Plane+Arc identity.

**Anti-references — explicitly avoid:**

- The generic "indie Mac app" dark site with a centered hero, a gradient orb behind a feature grid, and a "built for the AI era" tagline. Looks the same on every YC batch page.
- Notion/Vercel marketing-template feel: rounded cards in a 3-column grid, soft shadows, Inter, faint border lines, gradient buttons. We share zero DNA with that look.
- Glass-everywhere SaaS dashboards. We have one frosted card in the hero and that is the entire glass budget for the site.
- Any AI-startup tropes: cyan-on-black, neon underglow, "agentic," "intelligence," typewriter-cursor heroes, gradient text on every other word.
- Heavy feature grids with icon-over-heading cards repeated 6+ times. Our `ZeroFrictionSurface` is a 2×2 with intentional separators, not a feature wall.
- Marketing analytics, cookie banners, intercom widgets, third-party chat. Nothing in the hot path. The README explicitly defers analytics; keep it that way.

**Motion direction.** CSS-only. The hero animates the Plane+Arc once on load (`pa-frame-in → pa-arc-draw → pa-plane-in → pa-particle`, ~1.5–2s total). The Dynamic Island mock cycles three states every 2s with a `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` cross-fade. Everything else is static. Hover transitions are 250ms on the same spring curve. `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` freezes the Plane+Arc on its end frame and parks the Island on `completed`. Bounce, elastic, GSAP, Lottie — all banned.

### Design Principles

These are the five rules every future change to `web/` must pass. If a proposed change violates one, it does not ship without an explicit decision to override.

1. **Ephemerality is the brand, not a feature.** Anything new on the site — copy, motion, layout — should reinforce the idea that links die. Countdowns, decay, fade-outs, the Island lifecycle, expiry badges — these are all on-brand. Permanence cues (status pages, "join 10,000 users," "since 2020") are off-brand.

2. **Restraint over decoration.** The Plane+Arc mark and the violet-hot accent do the heavy lifting. New elements should subtract attention from them, not compete. If a new section needs three colors, a gradient, and a glow to feel right, the section is wrong, not the styling.

3. **Three typographic registers, no more.** Display Bricolage for headlines and answers. Mono JetBrains for labels, numbers, nav. Dim/faint milk for body. No serif, no script, no third weight system. Hierarchy comes from size and tracking, not from adding fonts.

4. **CSS-first motion, reduced-motion first-class.** No GSAP, no Lottie, no Framer Motion. Pure CSS animations + the existing 43-line `hero.ts` for state cycling is the entire motion budget. Every animation needs a `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` branch that lands on a meaningful frame.

5. **Speed-first as a stated value.** No analytics, no cookie banner, no third-party widgets, no web fonts beyond the two already loaded, no JS framework runtime. The site is Astro static + minimal islands and must stay that way. If a future ask requires bundle weight, push back or ship it as a separate route that loads on intent.

### Page-Specific Tone

- **`/` (landing).** Full marketing voice. Short, declarative, slightly dry. Story arc: hero promise → ephemeral premise → how it works → Dynamic Island moment → zero-friction surface → FAQ → footer. The TestFlight CTA is the natural exit, not the centerpiece — if the story works, the click happens.
- **`/pricing`.** Same voice, slightly more functional. Three tiers, mono numerals, coral ribbon on lifetime, no comparison checklist with 40 rows. Free / Pro / Lifetime cards stand on their own. Honest about the early-access window.
- **`/press`.** Same voice, more journalistic. Short product summary, founder one-liner, downloadable assets (logo, OG, screenshots), `press@fastsha.red` contact. Treat the press kit like an extension of the product — same fonts, same restraint.
- **`/privacy` and `/terms`.** Voice shifts to neutral and plain. Short paragraphs in body Bricolage at the same scale as marketing body, no marketing flourishes, no display headlines. Use mono only for section labels (`§ 01 — Data we store`). Currently placeholders; final copy lands before public beta.

### Accessibility Baseline

- **WCAG 2.2 AA at minimum.** Text on `--ink` background uses `--milk` (`#fafaff`) which clears AA at all sizes. Dim/faint milk variants must stay above 4.5:1 for body text — verify before introducing new dim tiers.
- **Focus rings are violet-hot at 2px with a 2px offset.** Already correct in `global.css`. Never remove.
- **`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` is honored everywhere.** Plane+Arc skips animation, Island freezes on `completed`, hover transitions remain (they are sub-300ms and not vestibular). New animations must include a reduced-motion branch.
- **Semantic HTML.** Section headings descend correctly (`h1` → `h2` → `h3`), nav uses `<nav>`, FAQ uses `<details>/<summary>`. Maintain.
- **No color-only signaling.** Coral expiry state in the Island is reinforced with the word "expired" in mono. Pricing tier emphasis is reinforced with the ribbon copy, not just the glow.

### Out of Scope (so we stop re-asking)

- **Light mode for the marketing site.** Decided dark. Do not propose a toggle.
- **Compression copy.** The product does not compress uploads (see `upload-compression.md` in memory). Marketing must not imply otherwise.
- **A second short-link domain.** The only registered domain is `fastsha.red` (see `domains.md`). Do not write copy or assets that reference `fsh.re` or `fsh.dev`.
- **Third-party analytics, chat, or cookie banner before public beta.** Explicit deferral in `web/README.md`.
- **A blog, changelog, or status page.** Not in the launch plan; would dilute the ephemerality posture.
- **Replacing the iOS app icon (sphere+arc+ghost) with the Plane+Arc.** The hybrid decision is settled (see `redesign-decisions.md`): Plane+Arc is the motion logo on web/Live Activity, the sphere+arc+ghost stays as the static app icon.

## Working Notes for Future `/impeccable` Runs

- The visual system is already strong. New work on this site is more often **subtraction and precision** than addition. Default to "what can we remove" before "what can we add."
- The PlaneArc component (`src/components/PlaneArc.astro`) is the single source of truth for the brand mark on web. Do not inline alternative SVG versions. Use the `framed` / `ambient` / `animate` props.
- Tokens live in `src/styles/global.css` as CSS variables and in `tailwind.config.mjs` as Tailwind color/font/animation extensions. Update both in lockstep when adding a token.
- The hero animation sequence (`pa-frame-in → pa-arc-draw → pa-plane-in → pa-particle`) is choreographed in `global.css` lines ~233–280. Touch carefully; the timing is tuned.
- Open thread to resolve before public beta: regenerate `public/og-image.png` from `brand/appicon.svg` (rsvg-convert) and replace the `#testflight` placeholder with the live App Store Connect URL.
